*Human haematopoietic stem cells remember inflammatory stress.*
Nature **655**, 458–467. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10522-7

Analysis/data archive: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18850871

The published coordinates, labels, LSI representation, signature statistics and underlying
